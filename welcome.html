SEVENTY–FIVE

Seventy-five days. Six commitments. Miss one and you start again from zero — and this app will be the one to tell you.

Open the app Free · no account · everything stays on your phone

The rules, enforced

  1. A structured diet geared to your goals — zero deviations, zero alcohol.
  2. Two 45-minute workouts, 3+ hours apart, one outside — proof photo required.
  3. A gallon of water, logged ounce by ounce.
  4. Ten pages of non-fiction — physical pages, audiobooks don't count.
  5. A progress picture, every single day.
  6. Miss any of it and the app sends you back to Day One.

Built like it costs money. It doesn't.

Proof or it didn't happen

Workouts and reading can't be checked off until a photo is attached. Honesty by design.

Scan your food

Point the camera at any barcode — calories and macros land in your log. A built-in library and web search cover everything else.

Your diet, defended

Log something that breaks your Keto, Paleo, or Vegan plan and the app catches it — with a better swap.

Do it as a couple

Bound together — if one of you misses a day, you both restart. Trade day codes over iMessage.

Watch yourself change

Daily progress pictures become a before/after and a full time-lapse reel at day 75.

Mind games that work

Your “why” shown daily, a don't-break-the-chain streak, milestones, weekly reflections, a nightly journal.
